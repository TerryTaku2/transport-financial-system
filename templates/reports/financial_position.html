Notes & Assumptions

  • This is a simplified statement, not a bookkeeping-grade financial position — the system has no cash/bank ledger and no loan or accounts-payable tracking.
  • Vehicles are depreciated straight-line over {{ useful_life }} years, dated from when each vehicle was added to the fleet (no separate acquisition-date field exists).
  • Cash & cash equivalents is cumulative net profit (revenue minus fuel minus maintenance) since inception — the same definition used on the Income Statement. It excludes driver/conductor commissions, which are not currently posted as an expense.
  • Owner's Capital is inferred as the total historical cost of vehicles, since no capital-contribution or loan records exist to say otherwise.
  • Liabilities will remain zero until loans or payables are tracked in the system.
