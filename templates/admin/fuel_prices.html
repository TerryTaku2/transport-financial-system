Fuel Prices

Current price per liter, used to work out cost and liters automatically on every fuel entry
Set the current pump price here and every new fuel entry uses it automatically: a cash amount logged in Daily Transactions is converted into liters, and liters logged in Fuel Logs are converted into a cost. This is what makes the Fuel Efficiency and Vehicle Efficiency & Profitability reports work without any extra data entry. Prices are local to this site and are not shared with other sites.
